📊 B2B Accounts Receivable & Working Capital Intelligence (90-Day Diagnostic)📌 Executive SummaryAn end-to-end financial analytics and credit risk diagnostic evaluating 90 consecutive days of B2B invoicing transactions across 12 corporate client accounts.   The objective of this project is to uncover liquidity bottlenecks, evaluate delinquent payment behaviors, and provide CFO-level strategic recommendations to unlock working capital trapped in overdue invoices and operational disputes.   🛠 Tech Stack & Relational ArchitectureData Engineering & Analytics: Python (pandas, numpy) — automated metric derivation, delinquency scoring, dynamic aging bucketing, and automated dimension table generation.   Database Engine: MySQL Server 8.0 & MySQL Workbench   Business Intelligence: Microsoft Power BI Desktop (Star Schema Modeling, Advanced DAX)   Data Sources: Structured CSVs (90-Day Enterprise Ledger with Delinquency & Dispute Scenarios)   Data Architecture (Star Schema)Dim_Clients (Dimension Table): ClientID (PK), ClientName, Industry, Region, CreditLimit_INR, PaymentTerms   Fact_Invoices (Fact Table): InvoiceID (PK), ClientID (FK), InvoiceDate, DueDate, PaymentDate, InvoiceAmount, PaidAmount, BalanceAmount, OverdueDays, AgingBucket, PaymentStatus, DisputeFlag   Dim_Client_Risk_Profile (Generated Dimension): ClientID (PK), Approved_Credit_Limit, Credit_Utilization_Pct, Overdue_To_Limit_Pct, Recommended_Action   Dim_Payment_Behavior (Generated Dimension): ClientID (PK), Average_Payment_Delay_Days, Max_Payment_Delay_Days, Discipline_Tier   Dim_Industry_Dispute_Summary (Generated Dimension): Industry (PK), Dispute_Rate_Pct, Disputed_Cash_At_Risk, Disputed_Share_Of_AR_Pct   Dim_Monthly_Cash_Trends (Generated Dimension): Billing_Month (PK), Gross_Billed_INR, Cash_Collected_INR, Collection_Rate_Pct, Cumulative_Cash_Collected_INR   📈 Python Analytics Engine & Risk ScoringData Cleaning & Fact Enrichment (run_analysis.py): Parses multi-format dates, reconciles missing values, computes outstanding balances, dynamically measures overdue exposure against a snapshot timeline, and segments transactions into standard risk aging buckets (Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ Days).   Credit Limit Governance Engine (client_risk.py): Calculates account-level credit utilization and overdue-to-limit exposure. Automatically triggers governance policies (CRITICAL: Place Immediate Credit Freeze, WARNING: Require Upfront Advance, NORMAL: Healthy Account).   Payment Settlement Discipline (payment_lag.py): Evaluates historical payment turnaround lag across settled invoices and segments clients into discipline tiers (Prompt Payer, Mild Delay, Moderate Delay, Severe Habitual Defaulter).   Billing Dispute Risk Module (dispute_risk.py): Isolates disputed transaction volume and calculates capital-at-risk percentages across industry sectors.   Time-Series Cash Conversion (cash_conversion.py): Aggregates gross billing against cash realization month-on-month, computing collection efficiency ratios and cumulative liquidity inflows.   📉 Core Financial KPIsMetricValueBusiness SignificanceGross Billed Revenue₹5.96 CrTotal transaction volume invoiced over 90 days   Total Outstanding (AR)₹4.25 CrGross uncollected capital currently outstanding   Total Overdue Debt₹1.40 CrCapital exceeding agreed contract due dates   Portfolio Overdue Ratio32.8%Aggregate delinquency rate (Industry benchmark: < 15%)   Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)64.2 DaysAverage cash turnaround time (Contract target: 45 Days)   🔍 SQL Analytical Queries & Findings1. Accounts Receivable Aging Breakdown (Query1.sql)Business Problem: Categorize open debt into risk buckets (Current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 days).   Core Insight: 34.89% (~₹1.48 Cr) of outstanding debt is overdue. While ₹96.65L is in the 1–30 day bucket, ₹47.23L has slipped into the 31–60 day critical delay bracket.   SQL Logic: Conditional classification using CASE WHEN and subquery percentage calculations.   2. Client Credit Governance & Limit Breaches (Query2.sql)Business Problem: Identify corporate clients exceeding 70% credit utilization with high overdue debt.   Core Insight: Meridian Enterprise Software (CL-107) and Apex Auto Manufacturing (CL-109) breached credit limits, operating at 143.95% and 140.90% credit utilization, with over 50% of their total balance overdue.   SQL Logic: Common Table Expression (CTE) with aggregate multi-table JOIN and threshold filters.   3. Payment Turnaround & Average Settlement Lag (Query3.sql)Business Problem: Evaluate historical payment turnaround discipline per client against contract terms.   Core Insight: Enterprise accounts systematically delay settlements by 5 to 9 days past agreed Net 30/45 terms, with outlier cases extending up to 48 days.   SQL Logic: Analytical Window Functions: AVG(DATEDIFF(...)) OVER (PARTITION BY ClientID).   4. Billing Disputes & Frozen Cash-at-Risk (Query4.sql)Business Problem: Quantify the volume of capital locked in active invoice disputes by industry.   Core Insight: Over ₹28 Lakh of working capital is actively frozen due to client billing disputes, heavily concentrated in the Telecom and Manufacturing sectors.   SQL Logic: Industry aggregation with conditional counting: SUM(CASE WHEN DisputeFlag = 'Yes' THEN ...).   5. Cash Recovery Decay & Cumulative Running Inflow (Query5.sql)Business Problem: Track month-on-month collection efficiency and cumulative cash recovery trajectory.   Core Insight: Cash recovery efficiency stood strong at ~48–50% in July and August, but dropped sharply to 13.8% (Sep) and 7.4% (Oct) due to stretched credit terms, widening the cash deficit.   SQL Logic: Time-series aggregation and cumulative running totals using SUM(SUM(...)) OVER (ORDER BY Month).   💡 Strategic Recommendations for ManagementImmediate Credit Hold: Freeze new purchase orders for accounts exceeding 120% credit limit utilization until their overdue balance drops below 80%.   Payment Terms Restructuring: Transition chronic late-paying accounts (average delay > 7 days) from Net 30/45 to Net 15, paired with a 2% early payment discount (2/10 Net 30).   Dispute Resolution SLA: Establish a strict 7-day cross-functional SLA between billing and logistics teams to expedite dispute resolution and unfreeze ₹28L in disputed funds.
